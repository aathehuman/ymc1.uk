Adult Alim Course

Structured Islamic studies for adults — learn the deen, step by step.

About the course

A part-time ʿĀlim course designed for working adults and students. Study the classical Islamic sciences at a manageable pace, taught in English, right here at YMC.

What you'll study

Arabic grammar, fiqh, aqidah, seerah, hadith and tafsir — taught from the foundations, with no prior knowledge required.

Who it's for

Adults of all ages and backgrounds. Whether you attended madrasah as a child or are starting completely fresh, you are welcome.

Timings

Classes run twice weekly in the evening. Final days and times will be confirmed with everyone who registers.

Where

Yardley Muslim Centre, 47-51 Stoney Lane, Birmingham, B25 8RE.
