Adult Alim Course

Structured Islamic studies for adults — learn the deen, step by step.

About the Course

A part-time ʿĀlim course designed for working adults and students. Study the classical Islamic sciences at a manageable pace, taught in English, right here at YMC.

What You'll Study

Arabic grammar, fiqh, aqidah, seerah, hadith and tafsir — taught from the foundations, no prior knowledge required.
