Yardley Muslim Centre

A place to worship. A community to grow with.

Yardley Muslim Centre exists to provide a welcoming local space where Muslims can worship Allah, learn their deen and strengthen their connection with one another.

We want YMC to serve people at every stage of life — from children growing up around the masjid, to families, elders, new Muslims and anyone looking to reconnect with Islam.

FaithKeeping worship at the heart of what we do.
CommunityBringing people of all ages together.
FutureInvesting in learning and the next generation.
