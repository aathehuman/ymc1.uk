Youth at YMC

Young Mu’adhin & Imam Pathway

Develop confidence in giving the adhan and leading salah under suitable supervision.

Training, assessment and responsibility

This pathway is for young brothers who would like to improve their adhan, recitation and confidence in leading salah. It is a development opportunity—not an automatic appointment as a mosque imam.

Adhan practice

Receive guidance on pronunciation, timing, confidence and the responsibilities of a mu’adhin.

Leading salah

Build readiness to lead suitable congregational prayers when approved by the imam or mosque leadership.

Recitation

Applicants may be assessed on Qur’an recitation, tajwid, memorisation and knowledge of the prayer.

Reliability

Punctuality, regular attendance, good character and willingness to practise are essential.

Please note: Completing this form does not guarantee selection or permission to give the adhan or lead salah. Applicants may be invited for a conversation and practical assessment. Any opportunity will be decided by YMC’s imam or authorised leadership and carried out with appropriate supervision.

Pathway application

Please answer honestly. It is completely fine to apply because you want to learn, even if you have not given the adhan or led salah before.
